# Barber Template Test Scenarios

## Complete Testing Checklist Before Launch

Run ALL these scenarios after wizard completes. Template must pass 100% before going live.

-----

## 🎯 CRITICAL TESTS (Must Pass 100%)

### **Test 1: Booking Intent Detection**

**Scenario:** “y’all open today?”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Detects availability intent
- ✅ Triggers lead capture workflow
- ✅ Asks for service first
- ✅ Does NOT spam multiple times

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "We're open [hours]. Want to book a time?"
User: "yeah"
Bot: "What service do you need?" ← Lead capture starts
```

-----

### **Test 2: Service + Time Intent**

**Scenario:** “need a fade at 5”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Detects booking intent immediately
- ✅ Starts lead capture
- ✅ Pre-fills service (“fade”) if possible
- ✅ Asks about day preference

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "Got it - fade. What day works best?"
```

-----

### **Test 3: Walk-In Inquiry**

**Scenario:** “walk ins?”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Explains walk-in policy
- ✅ Does NOT promise specific wait time
- ✅ Offers to book instead
- ✅ Does NOT trigger lead capture yet

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "Walk-ins welcome as capacity allows. For a guaranteed spot with zero wait, book online: [link]

Want to book a time instead?"
```

-----

### **Test 4: Pricing Question (Info Only)**

**Scenario:** “how much is a haircut”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Shows accurate prices from template
- ✅ Includes pricing disclaimer
- ✅ Offers “Want to book?”
- ✅ Does NOT auto-trigger lead capture

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "Classic cuts $30, fades $35, skin fades $40. [other prices]

Prices shown are current - final cost may vary slightly based on specific service details.

Want to book a time?"
```

**If user says “yes”** → THEN trigger lead capture

-----

### **Test 5: Location Question (Info Only)**

**Scenario:** “where are you located”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Shows complete address
- ✅ Includes map links (Apple + Google)
- ✅ Mentions parking
- ✅ Does NOT trigger lead capture

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "We're at [FULL ADDRESS]

📍 Get directions:
Apple Maps: [link]
Google Maps: [link]

Street parking available nearby!"
```

-----

### **Test 6: Cancellation Request**

**Scenario:** “can I cancel my appointment”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Shows cancellation policy
- ✅ Provides phone number
- ✅ Mentions confirmation link option
- ✅ Professional and helpful tone

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "[CANCELLATION POLICY] You can also manage it through your booking confirmation link.

Need help? Call or text us at [PHONE]"
```

-----

### **Test 7: Late Arrival**

**Scenario:** “I’m running 15 minutes late”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Shows late arrival policy
- ✅ Provides phone number to call
- ✅ Helpful, not judgmental
- ✅ Clear about slot availability

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "[LATE POLICY]

Give us a call or text at [PHONE] right now and we'll do our best to accommodate you!"
```

-----

### **Test 8: Barber Selection (CRITICAL)**

**Scenario:** “which barber should I pick”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ NEVER asks customer to choose
- ✅ Shares barber specialties briefly
- ✅ Redirects to booking system
- ✅ Mentions real-time availability

**FAIL Examples:**

- ❌ “Which barber would you like?”
- ❌ “Would you prefer Marcus or Tony?”
- ❌ “Who do you want?”

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "All our barbers are great! Here's who we have:

• Marcus - Fades & designs specialist, 8 years
• Tony - Classic cuts & hot shaves, 15 years  
• Devon - Beards & textured cuts, 5 years

You'll pick your barber when you book at [link] - you can see everyone's real-time availability."
```

-----

### **Test 9: Multilingual (Spanish)**

**Scenario:** “hola, cuánto cuesta un fade”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Responds politely (English OK if no Spanish support)
- ✅ Shows pricing
- ✅ Provides booking link
- ✅ Does NOT break or error

**Pass Criteria (English response acceptable):**

```
Bot: "Hi! A fade is $35. Want to book? [link]"
```

-----

### **Test 10: Full Booking Flow**

**Scenario:** “book me for tomorrow afternoon”

**Expected Behavior:**

- ✅ Triggers lead capture
- ✅ Follows correct field order
- ✅ Accepts info in any order
- ✅ Confirms at end with all details
- ✅ Provides booking link
- ✅ Mentions barber selection happens in link

**Pass Criteria:**

```
Bot: "What service do you need?"
User: "fade"
Bot: "What day works best?"
User: "tomorrow"
Bot: "Prefer morning, afternoon, or evening?"
User: "afternoon"
Bot: "What's your name?"
User: "Marcus"
Bot: "Best phone number to reach you?"
User: "555-1234"
Bot: "Email? (optional - you can skip this)"
User: "skip"
Bot: "Perfect! I've got Marcus down for a fade tomorrow afternoon.

Click here to lock in your exact time: [BOOKING_URL]

You'll pick your preferred barber and see everyone's real-time availability when you book."
```
